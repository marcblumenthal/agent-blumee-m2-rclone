Blumee Reader

A personal, read-only Gmail authorization for the Agent Blumee conversation system.

What Blumee Reader does

Blumee Reader is intended for the operator's authorized Agent Blumee mailbox. During setup it verifies the OAuth grant and mailbox identity. After separately approved native deployment, it may read incoming emails and threads to recognize requests, build context, maintain conversation continuity, and prepare responses for review.

The Reader connection cannot send email, delete messages, or alter mailbox content through its requested Gmail permission. Sending, when separately enabled, belongs to the distinct Blumee Reply application.

Requested Google permission

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

This scope permits reading messages, threads, metadata, and related mailbox information in the authorized Gmail account. It is a mailbox-wide grant, not permission limited by Google to individual senders, threads, or labels. The intended service applies separate operational controls.

Privacy and user control

The Blumee Reader privacy policy explains what information can be accessed, how it may be processed, where it may be retained, and how authorization can be revoked. Use of data received through Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Setup status: This informational site does not initiate an OAuth flow or activate production processing. Authorization setup and controlled production activation are separate steps.

Contact

For questions or a data-access request, contact agent.blumee@gmail.com or the application support address shown on Google's consent screen.

This site contains no inbox content, account credentials, OAuth tokens, or login forms.
