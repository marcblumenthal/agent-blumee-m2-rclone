Privacy policy

Agent Blumee Conversation Worker
Last updated: October 24, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy covers the current Gmail account-pairing functionality of Agent Blumee Conversation Worker, a personal integration operated by Marc Blumenthal. It does not cover the separate Agent Blumee M2 rclone integration, other Agent Blumee services, or future conversation features. The current worker is not a public account service or an ongoing general inbox assistant.

Google data accessed and purpose

With the account holder's consent, the worker checks the authorized mailbox's identity, sends one pairing challenge to the configured owner, and locates and validates the corresponding sent message and reply. It can access email addresses, message contents and headers, message and thread identifiers, labels, and timestamps for those operations.

The requested gmail.readonly permission allows reading the authorized mailbox's messages and settings, and gmail.send allows sending email on its behalf. These permissions are broader than the pairing operation. The worker enforces limits on its actions; Google does not restrict these OAuth permissions to the pairing messages alone. Completing pairing does not authorize future mail processing.

Storage, processing, and service providers

Google provides Gmail, the Google Cloud environment that runs the worker, and Secret Manager storage for OAuth refresh and client credentials. The downloaded Desktop OAuth client configuration is kept in a private setup directory on the owner's Mac. Neon stores limited operational records, including identities, provider identifiers, timestamps, hashes, and processing outcomes. n8n coordinates the workflow and receives limited operational results.

The worker is designed to process original email content and the raw pairing token in memory without writing them into its application database, ordinary application logs, or n8n workflow data. Application receipts and logs retain limited statuses, identifiers, counts, and hashes. Normal sent and received message copies remain in Gmail. This design does not guarantee immediate erasure from process memory or the absence of service-provider operational records.

The current worker does not send Gmail message contents or OAuth credentials to AI model providers or use Gmail data for model training. It does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising. Google, Neon, and n8n process the information needed for their respective services.

Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used to provide the account-pairing functionality described here.

Retention and removal

The current pilot's retention plan preserves operational evidence and encrypted backups through its seven-day pilot period and a thirty-day review hold after the pilot expires. Evidence about unresolved operations may be retained longer until reconciliation. These periods are review holds, not automatic deletion deadlines; automatic deletion is not implemented. The operator manages removal of application-held credentials, records, and backups.

The account holder can revoke the application's authorization through their Google Account connections. Revocation withdraws that authorization for future access; it does not itself delete existing messages in Gmail, application records, credentials stored by the application, or backups. Those copies must be managed separately. Contact the administrator to request review or removal of application-held data.

This informational website

These pages contain no sign-in forms, analytics scripts, advertising code, or embedded mailbox data. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses, when serving it. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of website visitor information.

Contact and updates

For questions or an access-removal request, contact the application administrator using the support email shown in Google's consent screen. This policy should be updated before material changes to the worker's data practices or the introduction of additional Gmail functions.
