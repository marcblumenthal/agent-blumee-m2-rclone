Privacy policy

Agent Blumee M2 rclone
Last updated: September 27, 2026

Scope

This policy describes the personal Google Drive file-transfer connection used by the application administrator on the M2 Mac. It does not describe unrelated applications or every service in the wider Agent Blumee project. The integration is not offered as a public account or file-storage service.

Google data accessed and purpose

After the Google account holder authorizes access, rclone can read file and folder metadata and transfer file contents. The requested Google Drive permission allows viewing, creating, updating, and deleting Drive files, except the separate application-data folder. The local automation directs operations to the owner's configured dataset, audit-source, and reporting locations; these configuration paths do not narrow the OAuth grant.

Access supports the owner's file synchronization, source-file maintenance, transfer verification, and operational reporting. It is not necessary to authorize this integration merely to read this website.

Storage and access

Transferred files are stored on the owner's Mac and in the authorized Google Drive account. Local operational logs and evidence may record filenames, file identifiers, transfer results, timestamps, or error details. OAuth authorization tokens and client configuration are stored locally for rclone's use and must remain private.

Google processes the data needed to provide its authorization and Drive services. Access to synchronized files also remains subject to the sharing permissions selected by the owner. Publishing these informational pages does not publish the synchronized files or change their Drive permissions.

Retention and removal

The owner controls retention of synchronized files, local logs, and backups. This integration does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period. The account holder can revoke its Google account authorization to prevent continued access through that authorization. Revocation does not itself delete copies already stored on the Mac, in Drive, or in backups; those copies must be managed separately by the owner.

This informational website

These pages contain no sign-in forms, analytics scripts, advertising code, or embedded Drive data. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, in providing the website. For GitHub Pages hosting, see GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and updates

For questions or an access-removal request, contact the application administrator using the support email shown in Google's consent screen. This policy should be updated before material changes to the integration's data practices.
