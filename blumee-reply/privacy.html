Blumee Reply — Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Operator and scope

Blumee Reply is a personal-use Gmail authorization operated for the Agent Blumee system. It is not a public signup service. This policy applies specifically to the Reply connection, not to other Agent Blumee applications or the separate Google Drive rclone integration.

Data and permissions

The app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. Gmail readonly permits access to the authorized mailbox's messages, threads, addresses, headers, labels, message bodies, attachments and related metadata. Gmail send also permits sending email on behalf of the authorized Gmail account. These grants apply to the authorized mailbox as a whole; Google does not restrict them to particular conversations or senders.

Purpose and processing

During account setup, the app is intended to verify the grant and account identity. After separate approval and deployment, the Reply service may use conversation context to prepare, send and confirm replies to authorized requests, and handle delivery status or uncertain-send reconciliation. No live message processing or sending is authorized merely by publishing this website or obtaining a consent grant.

Storage and service providers

Private OAuth client configuration and tokens are intended to be held on the operator's Mac during initial qualification and, after separately approved deployment, in access-controlled services such as Google Cloud Secret Manager. The hosted system uses Google Cloud, n8n and Neon for execution, coordination and limited durable state or evidence. Depending on the authorized task, selected message information may be processed by configured AI providers for response generation or review; this is not an unconditional authorization to transfer all mailbox contents. Data access must remain limited to the approved purpose.

Operational records may include mailbox identity, message/thread identifiers, timestamps, delivery state, hashes, and diagnostic outcomes. Authorized conversation content or attachments may be retained where required for continuity or processing; this website does not represent that all data is immediately erased or never logged by service providers. The production retention and deletion controls remain subject to separate deployment qualification.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used to provide the requested Gmail functionality, not for advertising or sale. Application use and transfer of Google API data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No data received through Google APIs is offered for generalized model training.

Retention, revocation and deletion requests

The operator manages application records, encrypted backups, and retention policies. A fixed automatic deletion deadline has not been verified for this new connection. The account holder can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access through that grant but does not delete messages already in Gmail or copies previously retained by authorized systems and backups. For a data-access or deletion request, contact agent.blumee@gmail.com.

Website and contact

This static informational website contains no Google login form, mailbox contents, OAuth tokens, or usage analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process normal visitor connection metadata. See GitHub's privacy statement. Contact agent.blumee@gmail.com for application questions. This policy must be revised when the app's actual functionality, sharing, or retention practices materially change.
